# ❓ FAQ

## What is this repository?

It is the public website for the Last Time We Hugged apps: a home page, the privacy policy and a contact and support page. GitHub Pages serves it.

## Does the site use cookies, analytics or scripts?

No. The pages are static HTML and CSS, and `make check` fails if a script, form or iframe appears. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep standard server logs, which the privacy policy says.

## Where is the privacy policy?

At <https://caputodavide93.github.io/last-time-we-hugged-site/privacy/>. The app's Settings and the store listings link to it.

## Does the app collect my data?

The policy says it does not: no account, no servers, no analytics, ads or tracking, and the journal stays on your phone unless you sync or send a file yourself. Read the [policy](https://caputodavide93.github.io/last-time-we-hugged-site/privacy/) for the exact wording and its effective date.

## Is the app free?

Yes, according to the policy: no purchases, no subscriptions and no ads.

## Can I use the pages for my own app?

The code is MIT licensed, so the layout and styles are free to reuse. The text is the Last Time We Hugged policy and describes that app only; write your own statements and have them checked against your own app.

## How do I report a problem with a page?

Open an issue with the page address, your browser and operating system, and a screenshot for layout problems. [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) lists what to include. A security problem goes through a private advisory, as [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) explains.

## Why is the support address not in the README?

The README and docs do not repeat it so the address lives in one place, on the contact page, where the app and the stores point.
