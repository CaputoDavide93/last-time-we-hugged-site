Common questions

How do I move my journal to a new phone?

On the old phone, Settings → Export backup and save the file. On the new phone, install the app and tap Restore from backup on the welcome screen. It works between iPhone and Android. The app keeps its data out of phone backups, so this is the way to bring your journal along.

I’m not getting reminders.

Check that Settings → Reminders → Unresolved argument nudges is on, and that notifications for Last Time We Hugged are allowed in your phone’s Settings. The Sunday check-in and the flashpoint heads-up stay off until you switch them on.

How do we sync our two phones?

Nearby sync is part of Pro on each phone (on iPhone, Family Sharing covers both). Put both phones on the same Wi-Fi. On one, open Settings → Sync with partner’s phone and show a code; on the other, pick that phone and type the code. It works between iPhone and Android.

I bought Pro on another phone.

Open Pro and tap Restore purchase, signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account.

Face ID or the fingerprint prompt doesn’t appear.

The app lock asks only after the opening animation, and only if it’s on in Settings → Privacy. If you turned off Face ID for the app in your phone’s Settings, the phone’s passcode unlocks it instead.
