Keep what you learn from an argument.

Last Time We Hugged is a journal for couples, on iPhone and Android. Note what set an argument off, how it ended and what you’d each do differently, then see the patterns as plain facts from your own log.

  • No account, no servers, no analytics, no ads.
  • Facts from your own entries. Never advice, never a verdict.
  • Your journal stays on your phone unless you choose to sync it.
